## Changes and design

Small modules own admission permits, request task lifetime, render-budget policy
and source-link presentation. Tests use their public interfaces. Zero-queue
admission precedes side effects; explicit cancellation invalidates browser
callbacks; progress reports only known facts. CPU scheduling is not substituted
for scene rendering. This separates correctness, efficiency and operator judgment.
